# Privacy Policy

<p class="last-updated"><strong>Last updated:</strong> 22/03/2026</p>

ShrugStudios ("we," "our," or "the Company") operates the Verbal mobile application ("the App"). This page informs you about our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Authentication Information

We use Sign in with Apple for user authentication. When you sign in with Apple, we receive:

- Your unique Apple user identifier (User ID)
- Your email address (optional, depending on your Apple settings)
- Your name (optional, depending on your Apple settings)

We do not store your Apple password. Authentication is managed entirely by Apple.

### 1.2 Game Data and Progress

To provide a personalized experience and save your progress, we collect and store:

- Puzzle attempts and answers
- Game completion times
- Scores and performance statistics
- Completed game streaks
- Game history
- Date and time of each attempt

All of this data is associated with your User ID and securely stored in our backend database (Supabase).

### 1.3 Subscription Information

If you subscribe to Verbal Premium, Apple processes your payment information. We only receive:

- Subscription status (active/inactive)
- Plan type (monthly/annual)
- Expiration date

We do not have access to your credit card or payment method details.

### 1.4 Locally Stored Data

We store the following data locally on your device:

- Visual theme preferences
- Subscription status
- List of puzzles played today
- Authentication session state

This data is saved in your device's UserDefaults and SwiftData.

### 1.5 Advertising Information

In compliance with Apple's policies, we will only access tracking identifiers if you grant explicit consent through the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt when launching the App.

The free version of Verbal displays ads managed by Google AdMob. Depending on your privacy settings, AdMob may process:
- Advertising identifiers: IDFA (only if tracking is authorized) or anonymized technical identifiers.
- Diagnostic data: Information about ad performance and unexpected crashes.
- Interaction: Ad clicks or views to limit repetition.

If you do not authorize tracking, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized and will be based solely on the App's content context.

For more information, see Google AdMob's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

### 2.1 Provide and Improve the Service

- Authenticate your identity and manage your account
- Save your game progress between sessions
- Sync your progress across devices
- Display personalized performance statistics and analytics
- Generate appropriate puzzles and content

### 2.2 Manage Subscriptions

- Verify your Premium subscription status
- Remove ads for Premium users
- Process renewals and cancellations

### 2.3 Advertising (Free Version Only)

- Display relevant ads
- Measure ad effectiveness
- Limit ad frequency per game

### 2.4 Analytics and Improvements

- Understand how users interact with the App
- Identify and resolve technical issues
- Improve puzzle difficulty and quality
- Develop new features

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for collecting and using your information includes:
- **Contract performance**: game data, progress, and authentication (required for the service to work)
- **Consent**: advertising tracking via ATT and personalized advertising
- **Legitimate interest**: aggregated usage analytics to improve the service

## 4. Sharing Your Information

### 4.1 We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personally identifiable information to third parties.

### 4.2 Service Providers

We share information with third-party service providers who help us operate the App:

**Supabase (Backend and Database)**

- Purpose: Data storage, authentication, synchronization
- Data shared: User ID, game data, statistics
- Location: Cloud servers
- Privacy policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

**Apple (Authentication and Payments)**

- Purpose: Sign in with Apple, subscription processing
- Data shared: Authentication token, subscription information
- Privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

**Google AdMob (Advertising)**

- Purpose: Displaying ads in the free version
- Data shared: IDFA, device information, ad interactions
- Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### 4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to:

- Valid government requests
- Court orders

- Protection of our legal rights

## 5. Data Security

We implement security measures designed to protect your information:

- Encrypted communications (HTTPS/TLS)
- Secure authentication via Sign in with Apple
- Row-level security (RLS) in the database
- Restricted access to user data
- Secure session tokens

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information while you maintain an active account to ensure game synchronization.

Your right to deletion:
Apple requires that you, as a user, have full control over your data. You have two ways to permanently delete your information:
- From the App: In the "Settings" menu, you will find the "Delete Account" option. Upon confirmation, all your game data, statistics, and profile stored in Supabase will be immediately and irreversibly deleted.
- By Email: You may request deletion by sending a message to penguinshrug@gmail.com.

Please note that deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active subscription (this must be managed through the Apple App Store).

### 7.4 Advertising Controls

You can limit advertising tracking:

**iOS:**

- Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
- Disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
- Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising
- Enable "Limit Ad Tracking"

## 8. Children's Privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.
