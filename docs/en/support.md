## 🐛 Report a Bug

If you encountered a technical issue in the app, help us fix it by providing the following information in your email:

1. **Device model** and **iOS version** (Settings > General > About)
2. **App Version** (visible in Settings within Verbal)
3. **Clear description** of the problem
4. **Steps to reproduce** the bug (if applicable)
5. **Screenshots** (if relevant)

## 💡 Useful Information

### Before Contacting Us

If you are having issues with:

- **Subscriptions or Payments**: Subscriptions are processed by Apple. For refunds or billing issues, please visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)
- **Deleting your Account**: You can do this directly from the app under Settings > Delete Account
- **Syncing Between Devices**: Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple account on all your devices

### What We Need to Know

To resolve your issue more quickly, please include:

1. **Device model** and **iOS version** (Settings > General > About)
2. **App Version** (visible in Settings within Verbal)
3. **Clear description** of the problem
4. **Steps to reproduce** the bug (if applicable)
5. **Screenshots** (if relevant - please attach them to the email)
